Türkçe

Last updated: 13 October 2026

This policy applies to the Online Ehliyet Dersleri app developed by Fatih Barış Şahan (bensahan).

In short

You can use the app as a guest, without an account. If you create an account, your progress is linked to it. We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, or track you across other apps and websites. The app contains no ads.

Data we collect

Account details

If you create an account: your email address and sign-in method (email/password, Google or Apple). With Google or Apple sign-in, the name and email those providers share. We never receive your password; authentication is handled by Google Firebase Authentication. Guest use only creates a randomly generated user ID.

Study data

The practice sessions and mock exams you complete: which answer you gave to which question, whether it was correct, time taken and date. This is used to calculate your progress, weak topics and review schedule; if you have an account it is also backed up to the server.

Information kept on your device

Your planned exam date, daily goal, reminder time and theme preference are stored on your device only.

Notification identifier

If you allow notifications, a push token (Firebase Cloud Messaging) is stored so that reminders can be delivered to your device. You can turn this off at any time in your device settings.

Usage and crash data

Anonymous statistics on how the app is used (Firebase Analytics) and crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics): device model, OS version, app version and error details. Your name or email is not added to these records.

The app does not access your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.

How we use the data

To create and protect your account, keep your progress across devices, choose suitable questions and reviews, send study reminders if you allowed them, and find bugs and improve the app.

Where data is stored and who it is shared with

Data is stored on Google Firebase (Google Ireland Limited / Google LLC). The database holding study data is located in the European Union (Frankfurt); authentication, analytics and crash data may be processed on Google servers in other countries. If you sign in with Google or Apple, the app communicates with that provider to authenticate you. Otherwise your data is not shared with third parties, except where the law requires disclosure to authorities.

Retention and deletion

Your data is kept for as long as your account exists. You can permanently delete your account and all your data in the app under Profil → Hesap → Hesabımı ve verilerimi sil. If you cannot access the app, follow the steps on the account deletion page. Crash reports are kept by Google for up to 90 days.

Your rights

You may ask to access, correct or delete your data, or object to its processing: info@bensahan.com

Children

The app is intended for driving-licence candidates and is not directed at children under 13. It has no user-to-user messaging.

Security

Data travels between your device and the server over an encrypted connection (TLS). Data on the server can only be accessed with your own account.

Changes

If this policy changes, the current version will be published on this page and the date above updated.
