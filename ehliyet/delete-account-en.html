Online Ehliyet Dersi · Delete Account

Türkçe

Last updated: 19 October 2026

You can delete your Online Ehliyet Dersi account and all data linked to it in two ways.

In the app (immediate)

1. Open the app and go to the Profil tab.
2. Tap Hesap.
3. Tap Hesabımı ve verilerimi sil and confirm.
4. For your security, confirm who you are once more: Google and Apple accounts see their sign-in screen again, email accounts enter their password.

If you use the app as a guest without an account, the same steps delete the guest record on our servers and your progress on this device; no extra confirmation is needed.

By email

If you cannot access the app, send an email with the subject "Account deletion request" to info@bensahan.com from the address registered on your account. Requests are completed within 30 days.

What is deleted

Your account (email address and sign-in details), your practice and mock-exam records, your progress and badges, your push token and all study data on this device. If you signed in with Apple, the sign-in tokens Apple issued to this app are revoked too. Deletion cannot be undone.

What is kept for a while

Crash reports that are not linked to your identity are kept by Google Firebase for up to 90 days and then deleted automatically.
