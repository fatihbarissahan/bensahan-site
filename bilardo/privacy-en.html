This policy applies to the Bilardo 3D app developed by bensahan.

In short

Bilardo 3D does not collect, store or share any personal data.

Data collected

None. The app does not ask for an account, does not connect to the internet and does not send data to any server. It contains no advertising, analytics or tracking tools.

Information kept on your device

Your game settings (language, sound, vibration) and your progress (level, rewards, weekly goals) are stored only on your own device. They never leave the device and never reach us. Deleting the app deletes them.

Children

Because the app collects no data from anyone, including children, it is safe for users of all ages.

Changes

If this policy changes, the current version will be published on this page and the date above updated.
