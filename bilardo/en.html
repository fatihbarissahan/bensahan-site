FAQ

Where is my progress stored?

Your level, stars and rewards are stored on this device only. Deleting the app deletes them too.

How do I turn off the aiming line?

Main menu → Settings → Aiming line.

The game runs slowly on my phone.

Main menu → Settings → Graphics → Performance.

Do I need an internet connection?

No. The game works completely offline.
