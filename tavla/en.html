FAQ

Where is my progress stored?

Your level, ranks and rewards are stored on this device only. Deleting the app deletes them too.

How do I change the language?

Main menu → Settings → Language.

The board looks small.

Turn the phone sideways; the board then uses the long side of the screen.

Do I need an internet connection?

No. The game works completely offline.
